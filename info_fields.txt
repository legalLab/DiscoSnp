INFO fields of <prefix>_haplotypes.vcf
=====================================

Written by scripts/disco_haplotypes.py (option -H of run_discoSnp++.sh and
discoSnpRAD/run_discoSnpRad.sh).

One VCF record = one variable site of a locus. A locus is a set of kissnp2
bubbles placed on a common coordinate system through their sequences (with
their lower-case extensions, kissnp2 -t/-T) and through the reads mapping
several of them (kissreads2 -phasing). CHROM is the locus (locus_N), POS is the
1-based position of the site in the whole locus sequence written in
<prefix>_haplotypes_loci.fa (lower-case extensions included), ID is
locus_N_POS. A locus can be written in the reverse complement orientation of
some of its bubbles: the alleles are then the complements of the alleles in
their fasta headers.

A site can be described by several bubbles (a site with 3 or 4 alleles is
found by kissnp2 as several biallelic bubbles; overlapping bubbles share SNPs),
so some fields summarise several bubbles.


Fields shared with the discoSnpRad clustered VCF (<prefix>_clustered.vcf)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ty       Type of variant. Always SNP (INDEL bubbles are not handled by
         disco_haplotypes.py; they stay in the usual DiscoSnp VCF).

Rk       DiscoSnp rank of the site: the best rank among the kissnp2 bubbles
         describing it (rank_ field of the fasta headers).

UL       Length of the left unitig of the best ranked bubble describing the
         site (left_unitig_length_ of its header), '.' if absent.

UR       Length of the right unitig of that bubble (right_unitig_length_).

CL       Length of the left contig of that bubble (left_contig_length_),
         '.' if kissnp2 was not run with -T.

CR       Length of the right contig of that bubble (right_contig_length_).

Genome   Allele of the reference genome. Always '.': the loci are not mapped
         on a reference.

Sd       Strand of the alignment on the reference genome (1 forward, -1
         reverse). Always '.': the loci are not mapped on a reference.

Cluster  Locus number, the N of CHROM locus_N.

ClSize   Cluster size: number of kissnp2 bubble paths in the locus (2 per
         bubble; synthetic bubbles are not counted).

Note: in the clustered VCF, POS = CL (or UL) + the P_1: value of the header,
which is a 0-based position. Its positions are therefore one lower than the
1-based POS of <prefix>_haplotypes.vcf for the same SNP.


Locus-level fields
------------------

NB       Number of kissnp2 bubbles describing this site. 1 for a biallelic
         site found once; more for a site with 3 or 4 alleles or a SNP shared
         by overlapping bubbles.

NX       Number of synthetic bubbles describing this site. Before kissreads2,
         'augment' writes every SNP of a locus again in the sequence contexts
         of the other alleles of the SNPs close to it (single-SNP "synthetic"
         bubbles): kissreads2 needs an exact seed, so without them the reads
         carrying other alleles nearby would not be counted. Synthetic bubbles
         are removed from the usual DiscoSnp outputs.

BUB      Comma separated ids of the kissnp2 bubbles describing this site: the N
         of SNP_higher_path_N / SNP_lower_path_N in the DiscoSnp fasta files,
         and the ID column of the clustered VCF.

NSITES   Number of variable sites of the locus (number of records with this
         CHROM).

PC       Placement conflicts of the locus: number of bubble pairs whose
         relative placement (from the sequences or from the reads) disagrees
         with the placement retained. 0 is expected; a positive value points
         to repeats or paralogs merged in one locus.


FORMAT, for reference
---------------------

GT       Genotype. Follows VCF 4.2: '|' = phased with every other site of the
         locus carrying the same PS (all the sites of a phase set, the first
         one included, are written with '|'); '/' = not phased: the only site
         of its locus, or a heterozygous site no read links to the other
         heterozygous sites of the locus.
PS       Phase set: position of the first site of the phase set, '.' when
         not phased.
DP       Sum of the allele depths.
AD       Allele depths (REF first). Lower bounds when the site is described
         by several bubbles whose counts cannot simply be added.
GQ       Genotype quality.
PL       Phred-scaled genotype likelihoods.
